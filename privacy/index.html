Privacy Policy

Animal Smash · Last updated 3 August 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Animal Smash has no accounts, no sign-up and no servers of our own. We never receive any data about you.

Your progress — coins, level, weapons owned and day count — is stored only on your own device. Deleting the app deletes it permanently. It is never uploaded anywhere.

What our advertising partner collects

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve them, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow it
  • IP address, and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions — which ads were shown, viewed or tapped
  • Basic device information such as model and operating system version

How Google handles this is governed by their own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices

Tracking. On first launch iOS asks whether the app may track you across other apps and websites. If you decline, no advertising identifier is used and you see non-personalised ads instead. Declining changes nothing about the game.

Consent. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent screen appears before any ad is requested, where you choose whether ads may be personalised.

Both choices can be changed later in your device settings.

Children

Animal Smash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date.
