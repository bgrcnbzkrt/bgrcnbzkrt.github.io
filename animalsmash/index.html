Animal Smash

A 90-second arcade smasher for iPhone and iPad. Aim the ring, let the hammer swing, clear the table.

The game

  • 90-second runs. Each one is a numbered day.
  • You only aim. The hammer swings by itself — no tapping.
  • Keep the chain alive. Hits landed close together build a combo up to 3×.
  • Six weapons, from a toy hammer to a sledgehammer. Every one upgradable.
  • Ten animals, from chicks to an elephant that takes some convincing.
  • No lives, no energy, no waiting to play again.

Common questions

My progress disappeared.

Saves live on the device only. Deleting the app clears them, and they do not transfer between devices.

There is no ×2 button after my run.

It appears only when an ad is ready to show. If none loaded, the button stays hidden and you keep your coins as normal.

Why am I not seeing any ads?

New players see none for the first several days. That is deliberate.
